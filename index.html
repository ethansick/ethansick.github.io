About Me

I'm a QA Engineer at NVIDIA's GEAR Lab, where I work on evaluating robot foundation models and on the infrastructure behind them. I joined the lab as a robot teleoperator, collecting training data by hand. Within a year, I moved into QA engineering and was tasked with automating policy evaluation. That work became ScaleEvals, which I co-led with Amitoj Sandhu and built end to end. It will appear at CoRL 2026.

I like finding the slowest, most manual part of a team's day and removing it. Beyond robot evaluation, that has meant rebuilding our lab's dashboard, building an agentic code reviewer, and keeping our robot video pipelines running. I'm also finishing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at Santa Clara University, and I build Rust backends and other side projects on my own time.

Publications

ScaleEvals: Scaling Generalist Policy Evaluation through Reset-Free Autonomous Play
Amitoj Sandhu, Ethan Sick, Omkaar Buddhikot, Nadia Laswi, Wesley Durbano, Nadun Ranawaka, You Liang Tan
Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), 2026

A reset-free protocol that evaluates robot policies through continuous physical play: a VLM proposes tasks from the current scene, a VLM grader scores progress, and PlayArena ranks policies. Across about 3,000 real-robot rollouts, it reduced active human labor by 98%.

Selected Work

  • ScaleEvals. Built the full system as main developer: AutoPrompter (VLM task and rubric generation), AutoGrader (90.7% agreement with human graders), and PlayArena (Bradley-Terry policy rankings). Ran it on DROID Franka stations and a YAM arm. Python, VLMs (Gemini), real-robot evaluation
  • Robot data pipelines for GR00T. Operate the Kubernetes transcoding pipelines that have processed over 1 million robot videos across every embodiment in GR00T, NVIDIA's humanoid foundation model program. Kubernetes, SkyPilot, Docker, Linux
  • Lab dashboard. Rebuilt the department's unmaintained internal dashboard into a CI-tested full-stack app, now used by about 200 people for robot control, evaluation review, and error tracking. React, TypeScript, Next.js, FastAPI, AWS EKS and RDS
  • Agentic automation. Built internal services including automated ticket management and an agent code reviewer that has handled 1,000+ merge requests, replacing manual reviews that averaged an hour each. Python, LLM agents, GitLab CI/CD
  • Robot hardware and CI. Built wrist-mounted camera systems for Unitree G1 humanoid robots, and CI tooling for automated robot testing. Unitree G1, CI tooling
